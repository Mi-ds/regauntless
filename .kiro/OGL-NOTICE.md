**Status: compliance stub. Must be completed and verified before any public/commercial

## What we use and why it's allowed

Regauntless implements game *mechanics* derived from the **System Reference Document (SRD)
v3.5**, released by Wizards of the Coast under the **Open Game License v1.0a (OGL)**. The
OGL exists specifically to let third parties use these open mechanics. Using the *mechanics*
(ability scores, BAB, AC, saves, hit dice, classes-as-rules, feats-as-rules, the d20
resolution math) is permitted under the OGL.

## What we do NOT use (hard rules)

- **No trademarks.** Never "Dungeons & Dragons," "D&D," or the "d20 System" logo/mark as
  branding. We describe the game as an **"SRD / d20 fantasy game show."**
- **No Product Identity (PI).** No PI monster names or setting content (e.g. beholder, mind
  flayer, displacer beast, githyanki, etc.). Only generic fantasy (dragon, skeleton, goblin,
  warrior, wizard, elf).
- The TTRPG-fan *association* is delivered via **paid talent** (announcer VO/likeness), never
  by borrowing marks.

## Obligations we must meet before release

The OGL v1.0a requires, among other things:
1. Include the **full text of the OGL v1.0a** with the distributed product.
2. Provide a proper **Section 15** copyright-notice chain (at minimum: the OGL itself and the
   SRD 3.5 copyright line for the Open Game Content we used).
3. Clearly designate our own Open Game Content vs. Product Identity, as applicable.
4. Use **no** Product Identity or trademarks of others (see above).
